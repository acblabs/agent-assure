# ADR 0023: Additive corrections to the S1/S2 development close

- Identifier: `ADR-V1-CLOSURE-CORRECTIONS`
- Amends: `ADR-V1-MAP` (ADR 0016), `ADR-V1-RELEASE-REGISTRY` (ADR 0021)
- Tickets: `V1-BASE-01` / `BASE-01F`; `V1-BASE-06` / `BASE-06M`
- Status: proposed for exact-digest human acceptance
- Approval mode: `maintainer_development`; `NO_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW`
- Author: LLM implementation agents under the maintainer's correction instruction
- Development acceptance authority: an explicitly scoped repository maintainer
- Operational support acceptance authority: an explicitly staffed `release_policy_owner`
- Authorization effect: none; no method, profile, release or deployment admission

## Historical subjects and current acceptance

This amendment leaves all earlier ADRs, schemas, forecasts, signatures and fixed
tags byte-identical. An accepted signature authenticates its exact historical
subject; neither a repaired validator nor this new prose changes that subject.
The generation-3 S1 closure and detached S2 acceptance in the retained capture
are real development evidence. A draft's `proposed_for_development` field is
not its external acceptance state. Conversely, a valid signature or recorded
successful verifier result does not waive a normative omission.

The correction close binds the historical result and closure commits, the v3
tag object, the exact accepted S2 bundle, this amendment, the corrected forecast,
the new support-policy proposal, and the new implementation and check evidence.
It is a successor record, never a replacement of the existing close. Human
acceptance must explicitly identify the permitted scope and remaining limits.
No private key, adopted estimate, competence assertion, signature or historical
approval is generated by an implementation agent.

## BASE-01F: complete planning rows without changing the method

Use the additive `agent-assure-delivery-forecast/4` wire representation for the
corrected `delivery-forecast/v1` projection. Historical generation-3 readers and
records retain their old semantics. The new producer and consumer validate the
complete fifteen-field roadmap row contract, including per-ticket confidence,
dependency assumptions, risk assumptions, current status and trusted issue time.
Every ticket and ordered release occurrence remains represented exactly once.
Shared method fields must be present in each row and equal the frozen global
method contract; aggregate engineering and reviewer effort fields must reconcile
with the selected occurrences. Duplicated fields are checked equal, not treated
as alternative sources from which a consumer may choose.

Confidence, risks, status and assumptions are explicit human planning inputs.
They are not inferred from an old estimate, a generic global confidence label,
a source-code inspection, an LLM's recommendation or the existence of a signature.
The producer binds their exact values and the separate maintainer acceptance
binds that producer and the complete planning-record digest. Missing or unknown
rows, unsupported values, stale source identities, changed occurrence order,
invalid intervals, inconsistent arithmetic or absent signatures block the
current planning close.

The frozen comonotonic aggregation method is unchanged. Its exact sums are
conditional on the declared common-rank dependence assumption. Engineering P50,
engineering P90 and reviewer P50 remain separate. Combined P50 is their stated
engineering/reviewer P50 sum under that model; combined P90 remains unavailable
without reviewer P90 inputs. These are subjective planning quantiles, not
calibrated confidence bounds, independent evidence or calendar commitments.

## BASE-06M: operational support requires a distinct decision

The new [support-policy-v2.json](../governance/v1/sprint2/support-policy-v2.json)
adds explicit security-backport eligibility and target semantics. It binds the
exact raw `SECURITY.md` digest and its existing
`agent-assure/security-vulnerability-intake/v1` policy. That intake contract
remains in force independently of this pending proposal: Critical human
acknowledgement is due in 1 elapsed hour and containment/no-supported-exposure
verification or safe-state entry in 4 elapsed hours; High acknowledgement is due
in 4 elapsed hours and containment/no-supported-exposure verification or
safe-state entry in 24 elapsed hours. The clock begins at the earlier of report
receipt and internal detection, runs continuously, and cannot be reset by
validation, forwarding, actionability, a new incident record or reclassification.
Credible Critical allegations retain Critical objectives until a named owner
records evidence supporting a lower severity.

The proposed 24/72-hour values are additional maintenance-coordination
acknowledgement targets, not replacements for security intake. The effective
Critical/High security acknowledgement objectives are the stricter minima,
`min(1, 24) = 1` and `min(4, 72) = 4` elapsed hours. Proposed patch-remediation
objectives remain 7/30 days and are distinct from the earlier containment
objectives. Only the actual policy owner can accept that added workload and
capacity. A stricter applicable, explicitly bound organizational policy wins;
this amendment has no authority to replace or weaken the production intake.

Preserve the existing 24/7 primary and independent fallback coverage. Critical
pages are immediate; missing delivery confirmation within 5 elapsed minutes or
any primary-route outage activates the fallback immediately. No human
acknowledgement within 15 elapsed minutes also pages the fallback. The fallback
requires a distinct person and durable route and cannot depend solely on the
same GitHub account, identity provider or notification path. Named people and
private destinations belong in the access-controlled incident-response system.
Public decision records may identify primary, fallback and accountable-owner
roles within `acblabs`, the stated operating organization, without publishing individuals' names
or private notification destinations. The signing owner must bind an
access-controlled roster reference and immutable revision and attest that the
roles resolve to distinct real people with the required coverage and independent
routes. The verifier checks the signed owner's separation attestation,
distinct role references, exact subjects and authority. The assurance level is
`owner_attested_not_independently_verified`; this does not claim that an
independent reviewer inspected the private roster. The owner-supplied roster
revision must be immutable rather than a mutable alias or placeholder; no
private-roster content hash is required in the public record. An authorized
reviewer can resolve the references through the access-controlled incident
system when an actual roster inspection is needed. The signed owner separately
attests that the referenced immutable roster revision is retained and auditable
by authorized incident reviewers; a disposable or unresolvable revision cannot
satisfy this requirement. An organization label,
two role labels or two notification aliases alone do not prove staffing or
independence. This proposal invents no roster revision, on-duty person or route
coverage. This is the existing `SECURITY.md` privacy boundary, not an exception
to its distinct-person or response requirements.
Missing coverage or missed objectives are recorded incident-control failures;
unbounded supported exposure must reach the documented safe state by its
applicable objective. Temporary risk acceptance under
`docs/security_release_containment.md` cannot authorize a release or publication.
The proposal does not assert that the owner is staffed or that a target is in
force. An S2 design-acceptance signature is not operational policy acceptance.

Operational adoption requires a separately typed, exact-digest
`sprint12-support-policy/v1` receipt with
`decision=adopt_operational_support_policy`, issued by a human explicitly
assigned `release_policy_owner` with `operational_support:0.7.x` competence.
Its signed claim records the accountable organizational role reference,
authority/trust snapshot, private roster reference/revision, capacity, backup,
conflicts, issue/expiry, supported line, exact policy bytes, proposed targets
and the statement that those targets become operational commitments. Missing
capacity, the signed real-person separation attestation, authority, signature verification
or exact subject equality leaves
the policy pending. Maintainer development cannot manufacture that decision.

Once adopted, receipt expiry is a freshness boundary for a new governance
decision; it is not an EOL decision or permission to drop existing obligations.
The line remains supported until the separately signed replacement and EOL
protocol closes. A missed response or remediation target remains a recorded
miss. A signed containment or revised incident plan must preserve the original
deadline and cannot relabel the miss as timely performance. Containment can
reduce exposure, but does not count as completing the maintenance release or
satisfying its remediation target. A critical incident
may halt a feature preview, but cannot bypass candidate qualification.

## Exact candidate installation before any publication

For each candidate, the credential-free verifier creates or clears its own
dedicated ephemeral environment with `python -m venv --clear <environment>`.
The destination is an explicit verifier-owned work directory, never a user
environment or a path chosen by the candidate. It runs outside the source tree
with user site disabled and `PYTHONPATH` cleared. It installs only hash-locked
dependencies from the declared offline wheelhouse and the exact verified local
candidate wheel, without rebuilding, repairing, fetching an undeclared artifact
or falling back to another index.

Before any signing or publication job for that candidate, the verifier runs
`pip check`, proves that the console script and imported package resolve inside
that environment, and asserts
`importlib.metadata.version("agent-assure") == candidate.package_version`.
CLI version, installed METADATA version, candidate manifest version and release
entry version must agree exactly. Bind the install-source wheel SHA-256 and
installed RECORD digest separately: a RECORD digest does not reproduce the wheel
archive hash. Persist the interpreter/platform, environment creation command,
commands, exact inputs, exit statuses and outputs in the candidate-bound install
receipt. Any mismatch or unavailable check prevents publication.

For public-preview read-back, `V1-DIST-01` downloads the exact wheel from the
declared production-PyPI project/version, verifies its origin, filename and
archive digest, and repeats the same clean local-wheel installation checks.
TestPyPI is optional publication preflight only; mixed indexes are forbidden.
Downloaded, installed or publicly visible previews remain nonauthorizing.

## Production-file scope and ownership evidence

The successor's production-file delta must be enumerated by exact base/result
commit and per-file before/after digest, with a purpose, applicable earlier
authorization if any, risk/compatibility assessment and validation evidence.
A new human receipt can accept the current corrected source for future work;
it cannot retroactively authorize an earlier edit. A missing earlier authority
is recorded as such, with an explicit current-source scope exception decision,
rather than invented history. Unrelated production edits or unexplained
conclusion-bearing changes block the correction close.

Record an actual inspection of `.github/CODEOWNERS` with source commit, raw
digest, command/exit status, owner-routing assessment and limitations. The
repository currently routes `.github`, source, tests, scripts, schemas, docs,
build and dependency surfaces to `@acblabs`. Pattern coverage is routing evidence;
it is not proof of branch protection, enforced review or an independent person.
Remote protection settings require separate evidence before they are relied on.

## Retention and Sprint 3 handoff

Retain the original capture at its original location, including the tested R3
checkout, C3, fixed tag, signed decisions, check evidence and original private
witnesses. Also prepare a durable, digest-inventoried local evidence export and
Git object/ref bundle. The export must exclude private signing keys and must
not publish the private roadmap. An export manifest describes what was copied,
what remained host-bound, and the original witness locations; copied bytes alone
do not establish portable replay. Do not move the original capture or silently
alter its tested checkout.

Create subsequent implementation in a separate checkout descended from the
verified C3, then apply and accept the correction result. Bind the durable
accepted S2 bundle and new amendment receipts in the handoff. Never dispatch
Sprint 3 against the stale pre-closure working checkout merely because its
source files happen to match R3.

The existing `v1-architecture-readiness` command reports draft integrity and
deliberately cannot establish detached acceptance. The correction close checks
the actual subject-bound signatures, current forecast, scope/authority, support
adoption, evidence and amendment dependencies. A recorded successful run is an
input to inspect, not a substitute for fresh trusted verification when required.

Before each Sprint 3 editing dispatch, the actual maintainer approves its exact
source/generated file scope, applicable design/amendment receipt digests,
occurrence dependencies, mode and work limits. Empty or stale scopes stop that
dispatch. `SCHEMA-01A` and `SCHEMA-01D` implementation-acceptance receipts must
close before dependent `TEMP-01A` implementation begins. No new implementation,
qualification, method admission, release or deployment permission is inferred
from this amendment alone.
