# agent-assure v0.6.4

v0.6.4 adds a deterministic, authority-scoped evidence-sensitivity protocol
for controlled RAG fixtures and hardens the trust, privacy, publication, and
distribution boundaries around evidence-carrying releases.

Highlights:

- adds self-digested corpus manifest, corpus snapshot, knowledge-authority,
  protocol, synthetic-data declaration, sensitivity-report, and process-equivalence
  reproduction-index contracts;
- adds offline `rag sensitivity` and `demo evidence-sensitivity` workflows with
  responsive, evidence-inertial, and wrong-direction deterministic controls;
- preserves exact retrieval, claim-link, fixture, subject, tool, and raw corpus
  evidence for both controlled arms while classifying confounded and
  prerequisite-unmet comparisons as non-verdicts;
- binds sensitivity comparisons to authenticated baseline and counterfactual
  RunSet digests and carries the result through comparison summaries, evidence
  packets, the assurance graph, and CI gates;
- adds privacy detector profile v2, including Unicode deobfuscation checks and
  exact-source redaction for reconstructed sensitive values;
- makes sensitivity publication exclusive and no-overwrite, with rooted,
  descriptor-pinned transactions and Windows handle-relative protections;
- revalidates typed graph, packet, comparison, sensitivity, and direct-gate
  inputs at public trust boundaries; and
- strengthens wheel and sdist verification with bounded archive inspection,
  exact wheel `RECORD` checks, source-equivalent payload checks, offline sdist
  rebuilds, and clean-environment installation verification.

## Upgrade behavior

Current package and persisted-schema metadata advance to v0.6.4. Existing
released schema snapshots remain frozen and replayable. Seven new persisted
roots belong to the v0.6.4 writer surface; historical artifacts are validated
against their own frozen schemas before compatible runtime projection.

The v0.6.4 comparison writer requires authenticated baseline and candidate
RunSet digests. Legacy unbound comparisons fail closed in CI by default; the
documented compatibility override is accepted only where it is necessary and
is recorded on the decision path. Packet CI can require sensitivity evidence
explicitly and treats sensitivity non-verdicts as invalid unless the advisory
override is selected.

Sensitivity output directories are immutable generation transactions. An
existing directory is accepted without rewriting only when all generated files
and bindings match the exact deterministic generation. Operators publishing
custom sensitivity inputs must provide the self-digested operator declaration
described in the controlled-sensitivity documentation.

## Verification boundary

The evidence-sensitivity protocol is a deterministic synthetic control. A
responsive verdict shows that the declared fixture path changed in the expected
direction under the exact two-corpus intervention. It does not establish
real-model prevalence, production behavior, semantic equivalence, causal
identification beyond the declared fixture protocol, regulatory compliance, or
external validation.

The process-equivalence reproduction index is a bounded inventory of committed
synthetic artifacts. Signature and digest verification establish identity and
integrity of the published evidence set; reviewers must still assess whether
the declared protocol, fixtures, authority scope, and acceptance policy are
appropriate for their use case.
