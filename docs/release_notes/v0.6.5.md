# agent-assure v0.6.5

v0.6.5 adds a protocol-bound, repeated paired evidence-sensitivity workflow
for stochastic live studies and hardens the source, privacy, filesystem, and
statistical boundaries of evidence-carrying releases.

Highlights:

- adds self-digested repeated-sensitivity protocol, statistical-sufficiency,
  and stochastic-result contracts for the narrow binary
  `expected_decision_response` endpoint;
- adds exact one-sided cluster-binomial planning and analysis under a declared
  independent-exchangeable-cluster assumption, with the fixed planned frame as
  the authoritative denominator and non-analyzable clusters scored as zero;
- prebinds immutable baseline and counterfactual live arms and fails closed on
  undeclared provider, model, tool, policy, or execution-configuration drift;
- binds every paired observation to canonical records in the exact source
  RunSets, persists privacy-safe source snapshots, and reproduces their
  membership and endpoint semantics during CI validation;
- carries stochastic sufficiency and results through evidence packets and the
  assurance graph, including exact candidate evaluation and comparison
  bindings for verdict-bearing evidence;
- introduces privacy detector profile v3 with stronger Unicode, structured-key,
  YAML-diagnostic, and inline live-credential protections;
- makes deterministic and stochastic directory publication crash-atomic at the
  target boundary, with exact-generation adoption and rooted identity checks;
  and
- adds bounded authored-input, artifact, cardinality, and finding-message
  handling plus safe Markdown rendering at public trust boundaries.

## Upgrade behavior

Current package and persisted-schema metadata advance to v0.6.5. Existing
released schema snapshots remain frozen and replayable. The v0.6.5 snapshot
adds repeated evidence-sensitivity, statistical-sufficiency, and stochastic
evidence-sensitivity roots while retaining the deterministic v0.6.4 contracts.

Verdict-bearing stochastic evidence now requires an exact satisfied sufficiency
artifact, protocol-prebound source arms, authenticated source RunSets, and the
exact counterfactual evaluation binding. Underpowered, incomplete, structurally
invalid, or source-inconsistent studies remain non-verdict. Version 1 of the
cluster-binomial method requires balanced cluster composition; the declared
maximum exclusion rate is an audit cap, not sample-size inflation.

Filesystem publication uses owner-only random sibling stages and an atomic
no-replace target installation. Exact concurrent generations may be adopted
idempotently; committed targets are never rolled back. Integrators that embed
the exact-statistics cache can clear it only at an explicitly quiescent
lifecycle boundary.

## Verification boundary

The repeated paired protocol estimates the declared response relation only for
the exact prebound study frame, endpoint, source evidence, and cluster
assumptions. It does not establish general causal identification, production
prevalence, semantic equivalence, regulatory compliance, or model behavior
outside that frame. The analytic exact p-value is authoritative; the
domain-separated Monte Carlo result is a reproducibility diagnostic only.

Independent statistical review is invited and has not yet occurred. Reviewers
must assess the coupling class, network-consent decision, planned frame,
exclusions, privacy boundary, and appropriateness of the declared assumptions
before relying on a stochastic verdict.
